## Risks
- Flattened image → editable layer reconstruction is inherently lossy; always provide fallback (locked original background + overlays) and surface confidence to users.
- OCR font recovery is approximate; do not claim exact font matching.
- Segmentation accuracy varies; provide manual mask editing tools or a review UI.
- AI costs can escalate — require mock mode locally and implement a credit/usage service early.
- Browser performance: do not load full-res images; use PREVIEW assets and thumbnails; lazy-load large assets and masks.
- Export fidelity: high-res export requires server-side composition; browser-only export may be insufficient for production-quality results.
